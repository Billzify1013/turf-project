FAQ

Common questions

Is TurfPro really free to start?
Yes — 100% free for the starter plan. No credit card needed. You can add unlimited turfs, customers, and bookings.
Does it work in Hindi?
Yes. The AI chat assistant understands Hindi, English, and Hinglish. Just type how you talk.
Can I edit a booking after creating it?
Absolutely. Change date, time, amount, or status anytime. If a new time conflicts, alternatives are suggested automatically.
How does dynamic pricing work?
Set rules with priority — Specific Date > Day of Week > Time-only > Default. Each rule can be a fixed price or multiplier.
Can my customers book themselves online?
Self-booking is on our roadmap. Currently, you (the owner) create bookings on behalf of customers. AI chat makes it 30 seconds work.
